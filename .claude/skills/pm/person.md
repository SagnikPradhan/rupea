# Who Rupea is for

Mostly salaried people in India, about 25 to 40, mostly in cities. They are comfortable with spreadsheet formulas, and rarely with ledger text. They note spending on a phone and review it on a laptop. Each persona below is named for what someone keeps track of, not for who they are. One person is often several of them.

The evidence is thin in places. Each persona says how strong its evidence is, and `research.md` lists the gaps.

## Writing about people

- Describe what people do and want. Never what they lack: no "not savvy", "lazy" or "can't".
- Assume no gender, age or family role. Write "the person who looks after the money", not "the husband" or "the wife".
- Use they and them.
- A persona is not a rank. People move between them, skip one, or go back.

## Investor

**What am I worth?** The priority persona.

- **Jobs:** see net worth across mutual funds, shares, EPF, PPF, NPS, FDs and bank accounts in one place. Review it monthly. Know the returns.
- **Triggers:** tired of updating a net-worth sheet by hand. Burned by a tracker app that was sold, redesigned or paywalled (Kuvera, ET Money). Wants no lock-in.
- **Must never:** lock their data in, or pivot into selling them loans or products.
- **Turned off by:** paywalls on basics, redesigns that remove features, wrong holdings.
- **Evidence:** strong. Spreadsheets lead net-worth tracking in both crowds sampled. SEBI 2025: 9.5% of households hold market products, 23% in the top metros, and 21% of investors find tracking performance hard. About 45 of Kuvera's 80 newest low reviews blame its redesign after the sale to CRED.

## Tracker

**Where did it go?** Its import work ranks as high as the Investor's.

- **Jobs:** see where the salary went, small UPI payments included. Categories that mostly guess right.
- **Triggers:** a first salary, a month where UPI spending ran away, quitting an app after typing every expense.
- **Must never:** make them type every transaction.
- **Turned off by:** effort, transactions counted twice, broken capture, ads, asking for a phone number, loan and marketing spam, losing their data.
- **Evidence:** medium. Spending is what most people track (about 80% of coded comments). Manual-entry fatigue is the top reason people quit. About 12% already use their card or bank statement as the log.

## Bookkeeper

**Show me the files.** The end of the ramp from plain text in `judging.md`.

- **Jobs:** read and write the ledger directly, fast. Import from other tools. Try new syntax. Script against the files.
- **Triggers:** they saw the lines a form wrote and found them easy. Or they arrive from hledger, beancount or ledger and want imports, linking and a web interface.
- **Must never:** break the file format, or hide the text behind the app.
- **Turned off by:** a format that drifts away from ledger and hledger with no reason, and features that work only through the app.
- **Evidence:** weak for arrivals (about 1 to 3% of coded comments use plain text). The ramp itself is a hypothesis.

## Caretaker

**What does my family have?** Parked: give it no work until the gaps close.

- **Jobs, in order:** if something happens to me, my family can find what I hold. Share money with a partner under a rule we choose: split, pool, or a joint pot beside our own accounts. Look after my parents' money without exposing mine.
- **Must never:** show one person's money to another in the family without being asked.
- **Evidence:** weak, partly hypothesis. The threads were chosen for the topic and for conflict, and nothing sizes the need. See `research.md`.

## Money in two countries

A situation any persona can be in: accounts in India and abroad, one net worth in rupees. Jobs are foreign statements, values across currencies, and exchange rates on the day money moved. Evidence is weak (vendor pages only), so it is a hypothesis.

## How people move

People tend to start as a Tracker and grow into an Investor as they save, sometimes keeping both habits. Some stop tracking spending once saving is automatic ("invest X, save Y, spend the rest"), so tracking must cost no effort to keep them. Any of them can become a Bookkeeper. The Caretaker usually comes later, alongside the others.

## Not now

Not now means we would do it badly today, not that these people are unwelcome.

- **Futures and options traders:** their tax rules and trade volume are a product of their own.
- **Freelancers and small businesses:** GST and invoicing pull Rupea toward accounting software.
- **A phone app:** a lot of work before the web app is done well.
