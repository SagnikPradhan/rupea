# Roadmap

What Rupea plans to build, in order, and what already works.

## Planned

- [ ] A page for income, month by month
- [ ] A page for what you owe on each loan and card ([#71](https://github.com/SagnikPradhan/rupea/issues/71))
- [ ] Set categories with rules, and change many at once ([#79](https://github.com/SagnikPradhan/rupea/issues/79))
- [ ] Go from your first statement to a net worth chart in minutes ([#80](https://github.com/SagnikPradhan/rupea/issues/80))
- [ ] Built-in schemas for more banks, cards and brokers, as sample statements arrive
- [ ] Import consolidated account statements (CAS) from CAMS, KFintech and NSDL
- [ ] Track EPF, PPF and NPS without typing them in
- [ ] Count transfers between your own accounts once ([#81](https://github.com/SagnikPradhan/rupea/issues/81))
- [ ] Report capital gains for an Indian financial year, and show which gains can be harvested tax-free
- [ ] Match refunds to charges
- [ ] Run Rupea on your own computer without signing in
- [ ] Back up and restore
- [ ] Send sign-in codes by email, with an email provider you set up ([#82](https://github.com/SagnikPradhan/rupea/issues/82))
- [ ] Sign up for Rupea hosted by us, without running anything yourself
- [ ] Use Rupea from other apps through a documented interface
- [ ] An app for Rupea ([#72](https://github.com/SagnikPradhan/rupea/issues/72))
- [ ] Track loans and the interest in each payment
- [ ] Add property, gold and other things you own by hand, with a value and a date
- [ ] Export as CSV, including the income statement and balance sheet
- [ ] Repeating payments
- [ ] Import ledger, hledger and Beancount files
- [ ] Receipts and notes on transactions
- [ ] Import scanned statements
- [ ] Statements sent by email or dropped in a Drive folder
- [ ] Search with a query language
- [ ] Budgets and goals
- [ ] Accounts in more than one country, with one net worth in rupees
- [ ] Create ledgers, and invite others to them
- [ ] Stay fast on decades of history
- [ ] Hear from more people who keep track of their money, beyond the investing forums, including people with larger portfolios and accountants

Not planned for now: futures and options trading, whose tax rules and trade volume need a product of their own; invoicing and GST, which would turn Rupea into accounting software; households with several legal entities, such as a trust or a company, which need more than separate ledgers; sharing a ledger with an accountant, which needs a read-only role on your own host; and tax advice.

## Works today

- [x] Read PDF, CSV and Excel statements, including password-protected ones
- [x] Teach Rupea a new statement with a schema, and review each import
- [x] Built-in schemas for HDFC Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank and State Bank of India
- [x] Keep your ledger in plain text files, and edit them in the browser
- [x] Browse and filter transactions and postings
- [x] Guess categories from past transactions
- [x] Link the same money reported by two statements, after reviewing both
- [x] Track shares and funds: lots, cost, prices, holdings and returns
- [x] Chart spending and net worth over time
- [x] Add, edit and delete transactions from a form, and see the lines it writes
- [x] See a home page with your net worth, a balance sheet and last month's income statement
- [x] See your accounts and your expenses on pages of their own, with charts and filters
- [x] Keep each ledger in your own storage
